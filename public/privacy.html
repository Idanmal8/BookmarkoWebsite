1. Who We Are

Bookmarko Inc. ("Bookmarko", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Bookmarko mobile application (iOS, Android, macOS) and the website at bookmark-o.com (collectively, the "Service").

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, when we share it, and what choices you have. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account data: email address, display name, and optionally a phone number when you register.
  • Social sign-in: if you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive a unique identifier and your email from those providers. We do not receive your password.
  • Profile content: reading preferences (favorite genres, reading goals), bio, and a profile photo you optionally upload. You may choose an existing image from your device's photo library or take a new one with the camera; the app requests permission before accessing either, and we only receive the single image you select.
  • Library data: books you add, reading status (Want to Read, Reading, Finished, Did Not Finish), reading progress, and favorite passages.
  • Imported book files: e-book files (EPUB) you choose to import into the personal e-reader. These files are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to or accessible by Bookmarko (see Section 15).
  • Collections: custom book lists, names, descriptions, icons, and colors you create.
  • Community content: posts (quotes, captions), comments, likes, and book reviews/ratings you author.
  • AI conversations: messages you send to our AI assistant, Ginnie, powered by Google Gemini.
  • Waitlist/newsletter: email address and optional name submitted on our website.

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

  • Usage data: pages and features accessed, reading streak activity, timestamps, and session duration.
  • Device information: device type, operating system version, app version, and language/locale settings.
  • Analytics events: collected via Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used in aggregate.
  • Crash reports: anonymized error logs to diagnose and fix bugs.
  • Purchase status: whether you have an active subscription; original transaction identifiers provided by Apple or Google. We do not process or store payment card data.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Book metadata: titles, authors, descriptions, cover images, and ISBNs retrieved from Google Books API, Open Library, and similar public catalogues.
  • Authentication providers: Google and Apple supply a user identifier and email when you use social sign-in.

5. AI Features (Ginnie)

Bookmarko's AI assistant "Ginnie" is powered by Google Gemini 2.0 Flash. When you chat with Ginnie:

  • Your messages and relevant excerpts from your reading library are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate responses.
  • We may cache AI responses temporarily on our servers to improve performance and reduce costs.
  • Google processes this data under its own Privacy Policy and applicable AI usage policies.
  • Do not share sensitive personal information (passwords, financial details, government IDs) in AI chats.

AI-generated responses are for informational purposes only and may contain errors. They do not constitute professional advice.

6. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Premium features are available via subscriptions processed entirely by Apple App Store (iOS/macOS) or Google Play Store (Android). We do not receive or store your payment card number or billing address.

We receive a transaction identifier and subscription status from Apple or Google to unlock premium features in your account. Subscription management, refunds, and cancellations are governed by the respective platform's terms.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account and library data for as long as your account is active. After account deletion:

  • Personal data is deleted or anonymized within 30 days.
  • Backups may retain your data for up to an additional 90 days before being purged.
  • Aggregated, anonymized analytics data may be retained indefinitely.
  • Data required by law (e.g., transaction records) is retained for the legally required period.

To delete your account, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account in the app, or email bookmarkohome@gmail.com.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit.
  • Encrypted storage for authentication credentials on your device.
  • JWT-based session tokens with expiration.
  • Firebase App Check / Apple App Attest for device integrity verification.
  • Access controls limiting who at Bookmarko can access your data.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at bookmarkohome@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected data from a child under the applicable age threshold, we will delete it promptly. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at bookmarkohome@gmail.com.

13. Cookies & Tracking

Our mobile app does not use browser cookies. It uses device storage (secure storage, local preferences) for session management and cached settings.

15. Imported Book Files (E-Reader)

Bookmarko's personal e-reader lets you open e-book files (EPUB) that you import from your own device. We designed this feature to be private by default:

  • Local storage only: Imported files are saved in Bookmarko's private storage area on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers, Supabase, or any cloud service we operate.
  • No access by us: We cannot read, scan, copy, analyze, or back up the contents of files you import. The text you read in the e-reader is not transmitted to Bookmarko.
  • Reading data: Lightweight data you create while reading — such as reading progress, highlights, and saved passages — may be synced to your account so your library stays consistent across devices. This is the same library data described in Section 2 and does not include the underlying file itself.
  • AI features: If you use the Ginnie assistant from within the reader (for example, to explain a passage), only the specific text you select or send is processed as described in Section 5. The full file is not sent.
  • Deletion: Removing the book, or deleting the app, deletes the imported file from your device. Because we never held a copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.
